Slide the menu. Keep the fight in the middle.

Click the mockup. The ☰ tab is a slide dock, not a 5×2 pad sitting on the action bar. Hotkeys 5–9 live on the right. The equipped moves and ball stay centered, and changing the ball grows the row left and right — not up and down.

← earlier HUD concepts

Menu

A tab on the right edge. Closed = one ☰. Open = a vertical list that slides over the world, then tucks away when you pick a window.

Hotkeys

Potions, mounts, extras leave the bottom strip. They become a right-hand column so the town stays visible under the fight controls.

Center island

Moves 1–2 · equipped ball · moves 3–4. Tap ⇄ and other balls fan beside the equipped one. Same idea if we later collapse unused moves.

Why left / right, not up / down

The current bars grow on the Y axis

Mobile / Amanda already grow the skill cluster up the right side. That hides the Pokémon you are fighting. A horizontal fan keeps the camera on the encounter and still shows every equipped option.

What this concept does not invent

Still no trainer HP or trainer EXP — those numbers are not in the save. The island is the active partner’s moves plus the ball you have selected. Trainer stays a menu page (name, money, badges, play time).
