1. Who we are

PokeVerse is a 3D Pokémon game played in the browser at play.pokeverse.quest: a non-profit fan project made by the PokeVerse team. Contact us on Discord or, inside the game, with Send to the team.

2. Accepting these Terms

To create an account, you tick the box saying you have read and accept these Terms and the Privacy Policy. When they change, the game asks you to accept the new version before you keep playing (section 12).

3. Your account

3.1 Your account is personal

Do not lend or sell your account: you are responsible for whatever is done with it. You may have more than one account; the rules apply to all of them, and using one to escape a punishment or to cheat is forbidden.

3.2 Password and recovery code

When you sign up, the game shows your recovery code once: it is the only way back in if you forget your password, so write it down. Changing your password or using the code gives you a new code, and the old one stops working. The team will never ask for your password or your code.

3.3 Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the game's account settings, with your password, or by asking the team on Discord. This removes the account and your character for good (Pokémon, items, Coins, Diamonds, progress, friends and messages), and the team cannot bring it back. Cancel any Market listings first: listed items are lost with the account. What we may still keep afterwards is explained in the Privacy Policy, section 5.

4. Community rules

In the chat, in names (trainers, Pokémon and guilds) and everywhere in the game, it is forbidden to:

  • insult, threaten or harass anyone, or spread hate or prejudice;
  • post sexual content, spam, scam links or anyone's personal information;
  • ask anyone for their password or recovery code;
  • pretend to be the team or another player;
  • cheat with a bot, macro, script or program that plays for you, or by tampering with the game;
  • knowingly exploit a bug (report it with Send to the team instead);
  • trade accounts, Pokémon, items, Coins, Diamonds or services for real money or anything outside the game: both sides are punished, and what was traded may be removed from both accounts;
  • scam other players in trades or on the Market (trades and sales are final once confirmed);
  • attack the server or break into accounts.

This list is not complete: anything that disrupts the game or other players can also be punished. And keep your own personal information (address, phone number, school) out of the chat.

5. Copying the game

Copying the game is forbidden: mass-downloading its files, mirroring it, redistributing it, reverse engineering it or running another server with it, even though the files reach your browser while you play.

6. The team and punishments

Team members have a label next to their name ([STAFF] or [MOD]). Depending on how serious it is, the team may warn, mute, kick, or ban an account for a while or for good, and take away anything gained by breaking the rules. If you think you were punished by mistake, ask for a review on Discord.

7. Diamonds, Coins and purchases

Coins, Diamonds, items, Pokémon and titles exist only inside the game: you can use them, but you do not own them, they have no money value and they cannot be turned into money or moved to another account. Buying Diamonds with real money is not open yet. When it opens, these Terms will get the rules for it (payments, refunds and disputes), and the game will ask you to accept the new version first.

8. The game is in beta

There may be errors, outages and maintenance without notice, and the game's balance (Pokémon strength, odds, prices and rewards) will change. A failure may make you lose progress, and progress may be reset; whenever possible we will tell you first, in the game and on Discord.

10. Liability

The game is provided as it is, with no promise that it will always run or be free of errors. We are not responsible for losses caused by your device or connection, by someone who used your password, or by deals between players. Nothing in these Terms takes away rights you have under the law where you live.

11. End of the service

The game may close for a while or end. If it is going to end, we will tell you beforehand, in the game and on Discord, whenever possible. When it ends, accounts and everything in them (Coins, Diamonds, items and Pokémon) stop existing.

12. Changes to these Terms

Each version has a number and a date. When these Terms change, we will announce it in the game and on Discord, and the game will ask you to accept the new version the next time you log in. If you don't accept it, you can't enter the game; you can still delete your account (section 3.3).
