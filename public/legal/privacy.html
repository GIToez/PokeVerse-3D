PokeVerse Privacy Policy

Version 1 — November 7, 2026

We do not sell your data and we have no ads. This is what we store about you, why, and who we share it with.

Contents 1. Who looks after your data2. What we store and why3. What other players see 4. Who we share it with5. How long we keep it6. Your rights 7. Children8. What stays on your device9. Changes to this policy

1. Who looks after your data

The PokeVerse team. Contact us on Discord or, inside the game, with Send to the team.

2. What we store and why

2.1 Account and game

DataWhat for
Username, e-mail (if you give one), password (stored only as a one-way hash, which not even the team can read), language, sign-up date, and when you accepted these documents (with the IP)Logging in, recovering the account and proving your acceptance
Trainer name, appearance, Pokémon, items, progress and where you are in the worldMaking the game work
Friends, guild, trades, Market sales and in-game mailThe social side of the game, and investigating scams

2.2 Messages

Public chat is passed on to the other players and not kept on the server. Direct messages between friends are kept on both accounts (the last 100 per conversation), so you can read them later.

2.3 Reports and diagnostics

Send to the team and I'm stuck send your text, your username, your trainer name, where you were and your device's diagnostics. To find bugs, the game also sends errors and loading steps automatically, with the browser, the screen and your username (or the IP if you are not logged in).

2.4 Security

To protect accounts and investigate cheating and scams, we record sign-ups, logins and failed attempts (with the IP), what happens in the game (catches, trades, sales, Coins) and the changes the team makes to accounts.

3. What other players see

  • In the world and in chat: your trainer name, appearance, the Pokémon with you, your title, guild tag and team label.
  • In the rankings and the Hall of Fame (open to everyone): your trainer name, title, guild tag and your numbers. If you hold the Champion's seat, challengers see your trainer name, outfit and the party you won with.
  • Your friends see whether you are online and where you are.
  • Nobody sees your e-mail. But if your trainer name is your username, everyone sees your username.

4. Who we share it with

Only what is needed, with:

  • the company that hosts the server (OVHcloud);
  • Google, which delivers the fonts on the screens and receives your IP when it does;
  • Discord, where the team receives reports and error alerts, with your username.

We also hand over data when the law or a court requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes the account, your character, your friends list and your copy of your messages right away (a friend you wrote to keeps their copy). Backups keep what was deleted until they are replaced by newer ones. Security logs, reports and the Hall of Fame are kept as long as needed for security and to follow the law, even after an account is deleted.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, receive or delete your data, or object to a use of it. You can delete your account yourself in the game's account settings; for anything else, contact the team on Discord or with Send to the team.

7. Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Players under 18 play with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, who can ask the team to see, correct or delete the account's data. The game does not ask for ID documents or a date of birth.

8. What stays on your device

We do not use cookies for ads or tracking. Your browser keeps your login session (up to 30 days, or until you log out), your settings for that device and some recent chat. With an account, your progress is on the server; clearing the site's data logs you out and removes the rest.

9. Changes to this policy

Each version has a number and a date. When this policy changes, we will announce it in the game and on Discord, and the game will ask you to accept the new version. If you don't accept it, you can't enter the game; you can still delete your account.
